Bulawayo Hoops

Week 5 Site Plan

Site Name & Identity

Bulawayo Hoops is the proposed name. A fitting domain idea is bulawayohoops.org or bulawayohoops.com.

This name is direct, energetic, and local: it tells basketball players in Bulawayo exactly what the site is for while representing the city's growing hoops community.

Site Purpose

Bulawayo Hoops will help players discover local basketball courts, learn pickup-game etiquette, and check in to planned runs.

  • A searchable court directory with locations, facilities, and local notes.
  • Clear pickup etiquette covering fairness, safety, and game rotation.
  • Interactive run check-ins that show who is attending and what a player needs to bring.

User Scenarios

Where can I find an outdoor court near my neighbourhood?
The court directory will provide locations, neighbourhoods, court features, and practical directions.
How do I join or organize a pickup run?
Run listings will show the venue and time, while check-ins will let players announce attendance and share extra details.
What rules should I follow at a new court?
A pickup etiquette guide will answer common questions about calling next, rotations, skill levels, equipment, and respect.
